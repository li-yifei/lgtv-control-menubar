# Third-party licenses

LG TV Control builds with [Go](https://go.dev/LICENSE) and includes
[MyGo](https://github.com/egoist/mygo),
[Purego](https://github.com/ebitengine/purego),
[Coder WebSocket](https://github.com/coder/websocket) and
[Cobra](https://github.com/spf13/cobra). Dependency versions are pinned in
[go.mod](go.mod).

The app bundle includes the license and notice files for linked Go modules in
`Contents/Resources/Licenses`.
